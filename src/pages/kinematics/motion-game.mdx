# Motion Match

Kinematic graphs represent the motion of real-life objects.  To build intuition for concepts like position and velocity, especially in the context of these graphs, try walking in front of an ultrasonic motion detector.


## What you need

**Vernier LabQuest Mini** (or LabQuest 2 or 3) connected by USB.
**Motion Detector** in the **DIG 1** port, with its sensitivity switch on the walking-figure setting.
**Chrome or Edge.** Other web browsers are not supported.

Put the detector at roughly waist height, with plenty of open space in front of it.
