## What you need

**Chrome or Edge.** Other web browsers cannot talk to a LabQuest.
**Vernier LabQuest Mini** (or LabQuest 2 or 3) connected by USB, with your sensors plugged in.

Until LabQuest analog support is finished, use the simulated sensors to try everything below.

1. Pick the channel to graph. Press **Zero** with nothing applied to the sensor.
2. Choose how long to record and when to start. A trigger waits for the signal to cross a level, and the half second before it is kept.
3. Press **Collect**, then **Stop** if you did not set a duration.
4. Drag the dashed handles to select an interval. The readouts and the line fit cover only that interval.
